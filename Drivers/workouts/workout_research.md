# Workout Research

## Ramped Workout

### Purpose
- Gradually increase the workload of the rider by progressively increasing the resistance (or incline, where supported) throughout the workout.

### Typical duration
- High-Intensity Interval Training (HIITT)/VO2 Max: 15-25 minutes.
- Beginner/Recovery: 15-30 minutes.
- Endurance/Calorie Burn: 30-60 minutes.
- Climbing/Advanced Intervals: 45-60 minutes.

### Resistance profile
- Starts at a low resistance.
- Resistance increases gradually at fixed intervals throughout the workout.
- The workout finishes at the highest resistance level.
- The increase may be linear or step-based depending on the workout design.

### Parameters
- Starting resistance.
- Resistance increment.
- Interval duration.
- Number of intervals.
- Total workout duration.

## Threshold Workout

### Purpose
- Improves the rider's Functional Threshold Power (FTP) by maintaining a high, sustainable level of effort.
- Increases endurance and the ability to sustain hard efforts over long periods.

### Typical Duration
- Usually 30–60 minutes.
- Multiple threshold intervals with recovery periods.

### FTP / Intensity
- Performed at approximately 95–105% of the rider's FTP.
- Includes short recovery periods between intervals.
- Maintains a consistent, challenging workload.

### Parameters
- FTP value.
- Number of intervals.
- Interval duration.
- Recovery duration.
- Total workout duration.

---

## Strength Workout

### Purpose
- Builds muscular strength and power by using high resistance at lower cadences.
- Improves climbing ability and force production.

### Typical Duration
- Usually 30–60 minutes.
- Includes repeated high-resistance efforts followed by recovery periods.

### Resistance Profile
- Uses high resistance during work intervals.
- Recovery intervals reduce resistance before the next effort.
- Repeats until the workout is completed.

### Parameters
- High resistance level.
- Work interval duration.
- Recovery duration.
- Number of repetitions.
- Total workout duration.

---

## Endurance Workout

### Purpose
- Improves aerobic endurance by maintaining a steady, sustainable workload over a long period.
- Develops cardiovascular fitness and increases stamina.

### Typical Duration
- Usually 45–120 minutes.
- Can be extended depending on the rider's fitness level.

### Resistance Profile
- Maintains a moderate and consistent resistance throughout most of the workout.
- Small resistance adjustments may occur to simulate changing terrain.
- Focuses on sustained effort rather than high intensity.

### Parameters
- Target resistance.
- Total workout duration.
- Optional terrain variation.
- Warm-up duration.
- Cool-down duration.
